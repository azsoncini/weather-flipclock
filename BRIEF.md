# Software brief

A clock face for landscape phone screens with a cyberpunk UI, implemented in a single HTML file.

Font: Orbitron (Black 900 for time, other weights for other elements)
Background: Pure black
Foreground: Pure white
Motifs: Cyberpunk forms. 45 degree lines.

- The screen should be divided in two horizontally.
- On the left side show the current time, large.
- On the left smaller show the date.
- On the right show the weather.
- The largest weather element should be the icon.
- Show the same information as in the printed weather example on the right.
- Use a sensible design heirarchy for things like font size, etc.

# References
